Cabin 33 °C → cooling. Off at 14:13 unless you say ac off.
14:03 · read back from the car

One line in, one receipt out. The receipt quotes what the car read back, not what was asked for — the AU 7X finding is that a setter returning true is not proof the car did it. Buttons carry a single-use token, so a week-old alert cannot start a heater.

Seat rule from the delta: cabin 33 °C with outside 29 °C means ventilation, never heat. Outside ≥ 20 °C vetoes seat heat outright, whatever the cabin says.

Honest limit: Sentry Mode off means the head unit sleeps, so nothing is read and nothing is heard. The settings row says so instead of pretending.
